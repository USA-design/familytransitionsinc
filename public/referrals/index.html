Make a Referral

Refer yourself, a family member, or a client to Family Transitions. Send a short referral below or call us, and our intake team will follow up.

Is this an emergency?If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988, available 24/7.

Who can refer

Anyone can make a referral, including the person seeking services. We often receive referrals from parents and guardians, schools, physicians, case managers, social workers, probation officers and community agencies.

Services you can refer to

  • Outpatient Therapy, Children · Teens · Adults · Families
  • Intensive In-Home Services, Youth ages 5–21 and their families
  • Adult Mental Health Skill Building, Adults 18+
  • DD Day Support Services, Adults 18+ with developmental disabilities
  • Sponsored Residential Services, Adults with ID/DD

Common questions

Who can make a referral?

Anyone: parents and guardians, the person seeking services, schools, doctors, case managers, social workers, probation officers, Department of Social Services staff and other community agencies.

What happens after I send a referral?

Our intake team reviews it and calls the person who made the referral to gather details and check eligibility. To confirm: Typical response time.

Can I fax a referral?

To confirm: Confirm the referral fax number.

Why doesn't the form ask for diagnosis or medical details?

To protect privacy, we collect clinical information by phone or through a secure process during intake, not through the website.

Send a referral

Please don't include diagnoses, medical history or other clinical details. We'll collect those securely during intake.
