Website privacy

Privacy Policy

How Family Transitions, Inc. handles the information you share through this website.

Draft for reviewTo confirm: FTI must review this policy with its compliance officer or attorney before launch, and link its HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send through our forms: your name, phone number, email address, the service you're asking about, and any message you write. Our referral form asks for a client's first name and last initial, and our job application asks for contact details, work history and an optional resume. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, diagnoses or medical history on this website.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, schedule consultations, process referrals and job applications, and, if you agree, send updates about our services. We do not sell your information.

Text messages

If you check the box to receive texts, we may send appointment reminders and updates about your request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing.

Health information

Information about the services you receive from us is protected under HIPAA and handled according to our Notice of Privacy Practices. To confirm: Link to FTI's Notice of Privacy Practices.

Cookies and analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our website. We do not use advertising or tracking tools on pages with forms. To confirm: Confirm the final analytics setup.

How we protect it

Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in systems with access limited to staff who need it.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Call (804) 745-5101 or write to Family Transitions, Inc., 620 Moorefield Park Drive, Suite 218, North Chesterfield, VA 23236.
