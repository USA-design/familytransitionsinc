Outpatient Therapy

Counseling for individuals, couples and families, provided in partnership with our sister organization, Core Behavioral Health, LLC. Our therapists look at the root causes of what you're facing and help you build insight, coping skills and practical tools for change.

What we help with

  • Anxiety and phobias
  • Depression
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Trauma and abuse
  • ADHD
  • Obsessive-compulsive disorder
  • Grief and loss
  • Anger management and impulse control
  • Behavioral concerns in children and teens
  • Self-esteem and body image
  • Substance use
  • Serious mental illness

How therapy works here

  • Individual therapy for children, adolescents and adults
  • Couples counseling
  • Family therapy that treats the family as a unit
  • Evidence-based approaches, including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)

How to get started

STEP 1

Reach out

Call (804) 745-5101, request a call back, or have a professional send a referral.

STEP 3

Begin services
