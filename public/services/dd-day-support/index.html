DD Day Support Services

Our DD Day Support center in Petersburg helps adults with developmental disabilities build daily living skills, confidence and connections in the community, with person-centered goals set together with each participant, their family and their support coordinator.

Who is eligible

  • Adults 18 and older
  • A confirmed developmental disability diagnosis, which may include intellectual disability
  • Significant limitations in at least three areas of adaptive functioning, such as communication, self-care or independent living

Program activities

  • Daily living and self-care skills
  • Money management
  • Shopping and community outings
  • Volunteering and social events
  • Recreation, sports, games and YMCA exercise
  • Arts and crafts
  • Health and safety skills

Common questions

Don't see your question? Call us at (804) 745-5101.

Who can attend DD Day Support?

Adults 18 and older with a confirmed developmental disability diagnosis and significant limitations in at least three areas of adaptive functioning.

How is the program paid for?

We accept the DD Waiver, Day Support Waiver services and private pay.

Can we tour the center?

Yes. Tours are offered Monday through Friday. Call (804) 733-3922 to arrange a visit.
