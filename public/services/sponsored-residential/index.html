Sponsored Residential Services

Sponsored Residential Services give adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities (ID/DD) a place to live in a caring family home, with a personalized plan that supports independence and community life.

Who it's for

  • Adults with intellectual or developmental disabilities, including autism
  • People who need daily support, full-time or occasional
  • Families looking for a home-like alternative to group settings
  • To confirm: Eligibility requirements, including waiver

What residents receive

  • A personalized care plan
  • 24/7 support from trained caregivers and our team
  • Help with daily activities, meals, transportation and personal care
  • Community activities and relationship-building
  • Funding through Medicaid and state programs for eligible individuals

How to get started

STEP 1

Reach out

Call (804) 745-5101, request a call back, or have a professional send a referral.

STEP 2

Free consultation

We talk through your needs, check eligibility and insurance, and explain your options.

STEP 3

Begin services

You're matched with a counselor or program team and services begin.

Common questions

Don't see your question? Call us at (804) 745-5101.

What is sponsored residential?

It's a service where an adult with ID/DD lives in the home of a trained, approved provider family, with support from Family Transitions.

How is it paid for?

Services are funded through Medicaid and state programs for eligible individuals. To confirm: Confirm waiver names and any costs to families.

Can I become a provider?

Yes. We're looking for caring individuals and families in Richmond, Petersburg and Chesterfield. See Become a Provider to learn more and apply.

How do I start the process?

Call (804) 745-5101 or request a call back online. To confirm: Referral and matching steps.
