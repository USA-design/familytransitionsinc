Intensive In-Home Services

Intensive In-Home Services bring counseling into the family's home for children and young people ages 5 to 21 who have emotional or behavioral difficulties, are at risk of being placed outside the home, or are returning home from a placement.

Who it's for

  • Children and young people ages 5 to 21
  • Experiencing emotional or behavioral difficulties
  • At serious risk of out-of-home placement
  • Returning home from an out-of-home placement
  • A clinical need from a serious mental health, behavioral or emotional condition that significantly affects daily life

What makes our program different

  • Small caseloads, usually four to five families per counselor
  • Treatment plans built with maximum family input
  • A focus on the family's strengths, culture and values
  • Skills for handling stress and crisis long after services end
  • Help connecting to educational, medical, social and legal services

How to get started

STEP 1

Reach out

Call (804) 745-5101, request a call back, or have a professional send a referral.

STEP 2

STEP 3

Begin services

You're matched with a counselor or program team and services begin.

Common questions

Don't see your question? Call us at (804) 745-5101.

What ages does Intensive In-Home serve?

Intensive In-Home Services are for children and young people ages 5 to 21 and their families.

Where do sessions happen?

Counselors work with the family in the home and community, not in an office.

Who can make a referral?

Parents, guardians, schools, social workers, probation officers and other professionals can refer a young person through our referral form or by phone.
