Adult Mental Health Skill Building

Mental Health Skill Building gives adults 18 and older one-on-one coaching and life-skills training in their homes and communities, so that mental illness doesn't stand in the way of stability and independence.

Who is eligible

  • Adults 18 and older
  • A diagnosis of serious mental illness, such as schizophrenia, recurrent major depression or bipolar disorder
  • Needs individualized training in basic living skills
  • To confirm: Current DMAS eligibility criteria, in DSM-5 terms, to confirm with FTI

What we help with

  • Daily living skills, meal planning and nutrition
  • Budgeting and household management
  • Medication education and keeping health care appointments
  • Finding and using community resources
  • Crisis support and coordination
  • Building social supports and independent living skills
  • Case management and discharge planning

How to get started

STEP 1

Reach out

STEP 3

Who is Mental Health Skill Building for?

Adults 18 and older with a serious mental illness who need individualized training in daily living skills to stay stable and independent in the community.

Where do services take place?

In the person's home and community.
