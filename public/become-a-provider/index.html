Become a Sponsored Residential Provider

Open your home to an adult with an intellectual or developmental disability, and help them live a full life in the community. You'll get training, 24/7 support from our team, and competitive compensation.

Why become a provider

  • Make a lasting difference by giving someone a real home and a place in a family
  • Provide care in your own home instead of a facility
  • Competitive compensation through Medicaid and state programs
  • Training before your first placement, and ongoing support from our team 24/7

What providers do

  • Help with daily activities such as meals, transportation and personal care
  • Encourage independence and involvement in the community
  • Build a caring, respectful relationship that improves the resident's quality of life

Who we're looking for

People with a caring nature and a stable, safe home in Central Virginia, including Richmond, Petersburg and Chesterfield. We especially welcome families or individuals who are patient, reliable and empathetic. Caregiving experience is a plus, but not required.

Common questions

Who can become a sponsored residential provider?

Individuals and families in Central Virginia, including Richmond, Petersburg and Chesterfield, who have a caring nature and a stable, safe home. Caregiving experience helps, but we provide training.

Do I need experience?

No. Experience with people with disabilities is welcome, but a willingness to learn matters most. We train every provider before a placement.

How are providers paid?

Providers receive competitive compensation through Medicaid and state programs. To confirm: Typical compensation range and how often providers are paid.

What does my home need?

A safe, stable home with a private bedroom for the resident. To confirm: Full home requirements and what the home assessment checks.

How long does it take to become a provider?

To confirm: Typical timeline from application to first placement, and length of training.

Will there be a background check?

Yes. Background checks are required for providers and adult household members. We collect that information securely later in the process, not on this form.
