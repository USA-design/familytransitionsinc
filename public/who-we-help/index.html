Who We Help

From young children to older adults, we support people at every stage of life. Find the group that sounds like you, and we'll show you where to start.

Children, teens and their families

When a young person is struggling with emotions or behavior, the whole family feels it. We work with kids, teens and parents together, at home and in the office.

  • Intensive In-Home Services
  • Outpatient Therapy

Adults living with mental illness

Support to manage symptoms, build daily living skills and stay stable and independent in the community.

  • Adult Mental Health Skill Building
  • Outpatient Therapy

Adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities

Day programs and family-style homes where adults with ID/DD build skills, friendships and a full life in the community.

  • DD Day Support Services
  • Sponsored Residential Services

Couples and individuals

Counseling for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, relationships and life changes, for people of all ages.

  • Outpatient Therapy

Professionals and referral partners

Schools, doctors, case managers, social workers and probation officers can refer clients to any of our programs.

Families who want to help

Open your home to an adult with ID/DD as a sponsored residential provider, with training and 24/7 support.
