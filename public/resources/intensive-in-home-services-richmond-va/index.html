Intensive In-Home Services in Richmond, VA: Support for Children, Youth and Families

When a child or young adult is struggling emotionally, behaviorally or mentally, the whole family can feel overwhelmed. Many families in Richmond, North Chesterfield, Petersburg and surrounding Central Virginia communities need more than occasional support. They need structured, compassionate help delivered where life actually happens: inside the home.

Family Transitions, Inc. provides Intensive In-Home Services for children and young adults ages 5 to 21 who are experiencing emotional difficulties, are at risk of out-of-home placement, or are returning home after an out-of-home placement.

Our goal is simple but powerful: help families build stability, strengthen coping skills, and keep the family together whenever clinically appropriate.

What are Intensive In-Home Services?

Intensive In-Home Services, often called IIH services, are home-based behavioral health services that support children, adolescents, young adults and their families during periods of emotional or behavioral difficulty.

Unlike traditional office-based therapy, Intensive In-Home Services take place in the family's natural environment. That lets counselors understand family dynamics, daily stressors, routines, communication patterns and safety concerns.

At Family Transitions, our Intensive In-Home Services are family-focused. They address the child's mental health needs while also helping the family connect with educational, medical, social, economic and legal supports when needed.

Who can benefit?

Intensive In-Home Services may be right for children and young adults facing serious emotional, behavioral or mental health challenges. A referral may be appropriate when a child or youth:

  • Is experiencing emotional difficulties
  • Is returning home from an out-of-home placement
  • Is at serious risk of out-of-home placement
  • Needs Intensive In-Home Services as a necessary treatment strategy

We serve children and young adults ages 5 to 21 who meet clinical criteria.

Why families in Central Virginia choose Family Transitions

Every family has strengths, and every child has potential. We approach Intensive In-Home Services with respect for each family's culture, values, spiritual life, emotional needs and right to make its own decisions. We don't impose values on families. We help them identify their own strengths and build on them.

Our program emphasizes:

  • Individualized support tailored to each family's needs
  • Small counselor caseloads, typically no more than four to five clients
  • Family empowerment through skill building and crisis-management strategies
  • Strength-based treatment planning with meaningful family input
  • Training and accountability for counselors to ensure quality care

This approach helps families feel supported, heard and actively involved in treatment.

Goals of Intensive In-Home Services

The main goal is to help children and families function better at home, at school and in the community. Our services are designed to:

  • Preserve the integrity of the family
  • Prevent unnecessary out-of-home placements
  • Connect children and families with the right community agencies
  • Help create an ongoing support system
  • Strengthen family coping skills
  • Improve the family's ability to handle stress and crises

How in-home counseling helps families

Because the work happens at home, counselors can help families practice new skills in real situations instead of only talking about them in an office. Services can help families:

  • Improve communication between caregivers and children
  • Develop healthier ways to resolve conflict
  • Create safer, more consistent routines
  • Understand emotional and behavioral triggers
  • Build crisis-prevention and coping skills
  • Connect with community resources
  • Support a child's transition back home after a placement

Eligibility

Eligibility is based on clinical necessity. A young person must show a need for services arising from a serious mental health, behavioral or emotional condition that significantly affects major life activities. This may include serious difficulty with relationships, repeated involvement of community systems because of behavior, or difficulty recognizing personal danger or significantly inappropriate social behavior.

Because eligibility depends on clinical need, families and referral sources should contact us to talk about whether Intensive In-Home Services are a good fit.

Serving Richmond, North Chesterfield and Petersburg

Families in Central Virginia are served from our administrative office in North Chesterfield (620 Moorefield Park Drive, Suite 218) and our Petersburg location (111 Morton Avenue). Counselors come to you, at home and in the community.

When should you make a referral?

A referral may be appropriate when a child's emotional or behavioral needs are straining the family, raising safety concerns, or creating a risk of removal from the home. Parents, guardians, case managers, schools, social services professionals, court service units and other referral sources can all refer.

To make a referral, contact the In-Home Services Director or Clinical Director by calling (804) 745-5101, or send a referral online.

Frequently asked questions

What are Intensive In-Home Services?

Intensive In-Home Services are home-based behavioral health services for children and young adults who are experiencing emotional, behavioral or mental health challenges. They support the child and family in the home and community.

Who is eligible for Intensive In-Home Services?

Family Transitions, Inc. provides Intensive In-Home Services for children and young adults ages 5 to 21 who meet clinical necessity criteria and are experiencing serious emotional, behavioral or mental health difficulties.

Does Family Transitions provide Intensive In-Home Services in Richmond, VA?

Can Intensive In-Home Services help prevent out-of-home placement?

Yes. One of the program's goals is to prevent unnecessary out-of-home placements and preserve the family whenever clinically appropriate.
