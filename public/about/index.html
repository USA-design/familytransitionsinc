About Family Transitions

We help individuals and families live healthy, fulfilling lives at home and in their communities. For more than 20 years, we've provided behavioral health and developmental disability services across Central Virginia.

Home › About

Our mission

Family Transitions, Inc. believes the family is at the center of each person's well-being. We identify youth, adults and families who are at risk and provide services that strengthen families and help people stay in their homes and communities.

That work can mean reuniting families, setting healthy boundaries, reducing problem behaviors, stepping in at school when needed, and supporting families over the long term. Through community-based therapeutic services, we help people build a healthier sense of self, stronger coping skills and greater independence.

Our story

Family Transitions began in 2003 as a small agency offering intensive in-home services. Today we provide five programs, from outpatient therapy to sponsored residential homes, for children, teens, adults and families in Richmond, Petersburg and the surrounding area. We focus on high-quality, cost-effective care, community participation, and hiring from the communities we serve.
